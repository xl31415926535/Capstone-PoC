Student question

Aisha makes a circuit with one battery, one bulb and two gaps, X and Y, in the same loop. She has three strips, P, Q and R. Each strip is either a good electrical conductor or an electrical insulator. In each test, she places one strip across X and another across Y. Each strip touches both wire ends securely. The battery, bulb and wires work properly in both tests. Apart from the strips shown below, the circuit is unchanged.

A single loop with one battery and one bulb. Two gaps, X and Y, lie in series in the same loop. A strip bridges each gap. There are no branches or bypass wires.

TestGap XGap YBulb
1PRlights
2RQdoes not light

Which strips are electrical conductors?

  1. Q and R only
  2. P and R only
  3. P, Q and R
  4. P only

Answer and distractors

Option 2. The bulb lights in Test 1, so both P and R are electrical conductors. In Test 2, R is already known to be a conductor, but the bulb does not light. Since the components work properly and the contacts are secure, Q must be an electrical insulator. It prevents current from flowing around the loop. Therefore, P and R only are electrical conductors.

  • Option 1: Classifies P as an insulator despite the bulb lighting in Test 1, and classifies both Q and R as conductors despite the bulb not lighting in Test 2.
  • Option 3: If all three strips were conductors, the bulb would light in both tests. This contradicts Test 2.
  • Option 4: Classifies R as an insulator even though both P and R must conduct electricity for the bulb to light in Test 1.
